# `_shared/messaging.md` — the `Answer:` block is the human's

New section "Asking the human (`mail/out/`)".

A `mail/out/` question must end with a bare `Answer:` line and nothing after it. Put your own
text *above* it. Writing under `Answer:` makes a waiting question look answered and buries it in
the human's queue.
