# Historical records

These files preserve useful evidence from the stopped legacy team without reviving its runtime protocol.

- `incidents/` — orchestration failure evidence, mitigations, and raw uptime events.
- `task-sources/open-legacy-backlog/` — original reports behind the 20 migrated open cards.
- `retrospectives/` — observations about team flow and prompt/process behavior.
- `product-evolution/` — implementation narrative predating the office-space migration.
- `legacy-epics/`, `legacy-process/`, and `superseded-specs/` — previously migrated planning and policy history.

Historical statements may be stale. Approved product truth remains in `../spec/`; current team policy remains in `../WORKFLOW.md`.
